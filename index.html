Research

Jan–May 2026

AI and contract law

Researcher · Seton Hall University

Conducted independent legal research with Professor Katri Nousiainen on how autonomous AI agents form contracts and what makes those agreements enforceable.

Authored Consent Without Consciousness: Agentic AI, Autonomous Contract Formation, and the Failure of Existing Legal Frameworks. Proposed a tiered consent framework that adjusts enforceability based on AI autonomy, the materiality of contract terms, and mechanisms for pre-commitment or ratification.
